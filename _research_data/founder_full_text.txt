The Beginning • Queens, NYC
Vivek Thapar started Mirch Media in 2006 with a laptop, an internet connection, and a firm belief that businesses deserved better than marketing agencies that over-promised and under-delivered. What began as a one-person freelance operation building websites in Queens, New York, has grown into a full-service agency that has served 16+ client engagements across 16+ industries.
Firsthand Business Experience
As a serial entrepreneur, Vivek has launched multiple successful ventures across technology, media, and professional services. He understands what it feels like to bet your livelihood on an idea — because he&#039;s done it. That firsthand experience as a business owner is what makes Mirch Media different: we don&#039;t just do marketing, we&#039;ve built the businesses that marketing needs to support.
